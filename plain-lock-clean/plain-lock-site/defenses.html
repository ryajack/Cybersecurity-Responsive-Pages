Everyday defenses

None of these take long, and together they close the doors attackers use most.

Two-step sign-in

Turn it on for email first. A stolen password alone then stops working.

A password manager

One unique password per site means one breach cannot unlock the rest.

Updates on

Updates patch the holes that malware relies on. Let them install automatically.

Backups

Keep a copy of important files somewhere ransomware cannot reach.

If something goes wrong
  1. Disconnect the device from the internet.
  2. Change the password for the affected account from a different device.
  3. Tell your school or workplace IT team right away.
  4. Watch your accounts for activity you do not recognize.
