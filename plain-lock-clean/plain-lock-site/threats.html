Common threats

Four kinds of attack account for much of what students and small teams run into.

Phishing

A message that pretends to be your bank, school, or boss and pushes you to click a link or hand over a login. Look for urgency, odd sender addresses, and links that do not match their label.

Ransomware

Malicious software that locks your files and demands payment to unlock them. It usually arrives through an attachment or an unpatched program. Offline backups are the best protection.

Malware

Any software built to harm or spy, including keyloggers and trojans. It often hides inside pirated downloads or fake browser extensions.

Social engineering

Tricking a person instead of a computer. A caller claiming to be IT support who asks for your password is a classic example. Real support never needs it.
